Your questions

Full House Rewires FAQs.

Need advice about your property? Speak to Finn.

What is involved in a full rewire?

A full rewire replaces all existing wiring, switches, sockets, and consumer unit protection, ensuring the entire property complies with modern 18th Edition BS 7671 electrical safety standards.

Will walls and floors be disturbed?

Access to cable routes can involve lifting floorboards and chasing walls. The quote should make clear what is involved and whether plastering or decoration is included.

How long will it take?

Property size, access and the specification affect the programme. A proposed schedule can be discussed after the survey.

What affects the price?

The number of rooms and electrical points, cable access, consumer unit specification and additional circuits all affect the quote.
