Common Scenarios

When is a partial rewire the best choice?

If your existing cables are PVC insulated in good condition, you rarely need a full house teardown. A partial rewire isolates the specific problem area.

Kitchen Refits & High-Draw Appliances

Modern kitchens require dedicated circuits for induction hobs, ovens, dishwashers, and multiple worktop appliances. We run clean dedicated lines directly to the consumer unit.

Home Extensions & Knock-Throughs

Extending an existing ring main or running a fresh sub-board for open-plan living spaces without tearing up upstairs flooring.

Single Floor or Room Renovations

When refurbishing an upstairs bedroom, bathroom, or loft conversion while the rest of the home remains completely occupied and undisturbed.

Garages, Workshops & Outbuildings

Armoured SWA cable run underground or overhead with a mini garage consumer unit for power tools, lighting, and chargers.
