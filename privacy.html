Privacy Policy

How FG Electrical handles enquiry details, customer privacy, and data protection across Cumbria.

1. Introduction & Who We Are

FG Electrical (Finn Graham) operates electrical services across Cumbria. We are committed to maintaining the trust of our customers. This privacy policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is kept secure, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018.

Data Controller: Finn Graham trading as FG Electrical, Wigton, Cumbria.
Telephone: 07495 333990
Email: finngraham87@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

When you contact us to request an electrical quote, survey, advice, or booked installation, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your telephone number
  • Your email address
  • Your property address or postcode / town
  • Details about your property, scope of electrical work, or photos you share with us to prepare an estimate

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are collected and processed purely for legitimate business and contractual purposes:

  • To respond to your initial enquiry by phone, email, or WhatsApp
  • To arrange property surveys and provide written electrical estimates
  • To carry out agreed electrical installations, rewires, testing, or repairs
  • To register notifiable works with Building Control via NAPIT (Competent Person Scheme) and issue your Electrical Installation Certificates (EIC) or Condition Reports (EICR)

4. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal details with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Information is only shared where strictly necessary to fulfill statutory requirements or legal obligations:

  • NAPIT / Local Authority Building Control: To lodge formal notification of Part P notifiable domestic electrical work as required by Building Regulations.

5. Data Storage and Security

All customer enquiry information and job records are kept securely on password-protected and encrypted devices accessible only by Finn Graham. We retain customer records only as long as necessary to complete agreed work, maintain warranty/certificate records, and comply with UK accounting and electrical safety regulations.

6. Your Data Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have rights including:

  • Right of access: You can ask for copies of your personal information.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to rectify information you think is inaccurate.
  • Right to erasure: You can ask us to erase your personal information in certain circumstances.

If you have any questions or wish to exercise any of your rights, please email finngraham87@gmail.com or call 07495 333990.
